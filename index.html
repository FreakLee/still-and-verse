How it works

Choose a feeling, or describe your moment in a sentence. Every line comes from a small hand-checked library. Each result keeps the original Chinese, an interpretive English rendering, a short account of its context, and a link to the source text together.

Where Claude comes in

If you add an Anthropic API key, your words go to Claude together with a shortlist of matching lines. Claude chooses one and writes a short note on why it fits. It never writes or changes the verse, translation, or source. Without a key, lines are matched by the words you use.

Make it yours

Add a photo if you like. It is previewed and used to make your downloadable card in your browser; this site does not upload or store it.
